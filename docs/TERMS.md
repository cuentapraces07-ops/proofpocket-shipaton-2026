# ProofPocket Terms (prototype)

Last updated: 2026-09-18

ProofPocket is a prototype for organizing opportunity notes, evidence checkpoints,
and payout-readiness reports. It does not promise a job, prize, reimbursement,
payment, or eligibility for any program.

The RevenueCat configuration currently uses the Test Store sandbox. Any purchase
shown in the prototype is a test transaction with no real charge. Production
store products, billing, taxes, refunds, and legal terms must be configured and
reviewed by the account owner before a public release.

The prototype stores opportunity and evidence data locally on the device. Do not
enter passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, or other sensitive
information into the prototype.
