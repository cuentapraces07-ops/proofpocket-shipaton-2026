## Evidence and caveats

- RevenueCat's official [Shipaton site](https://www.shipaton.com/) says the
  total prize pool is $740k+ and that submissions close on 30 September. Its
  [Devpost rules](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules) govern
  eligibility, judging and prize paperwork.
- The current official rules require the first public release of the app during
  the submission window on the App Store, Google Play Store, or Samsung Galaxy
  Store, plus a working RevenueCat purchase or RevenueCat Ads integration. The
  demo-and-public-source exception is limited to the Next Gen Award for
  qualifying active students; no qualifying academic email is verified here.
- The [Amazon Developer Hackathon rules](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules)
  set the deadline at 23 October 2026 and list the Alexa+ track prizes as
  US$25,000, US$15,000 and US$4,000; the AWS Builder and Open Source mini-
  challenges list US$5,000 each. The rules accept a self-hosted MCP server or
  a clearly demonstrated Alexa+ simulation, but still require public source,
  a public video under three minutes and a complete submission.
- Emergent's [official Builder Fest page](https://emergent.sh/ai-contests/kevin-oleary-emergent-builder-fest)
  lists the deadline, award bands and payment-compliance requirements.
- The [Open Collective security policy](https://github.com/OpenCollective/OpenCollective/security/policy)
  explicitly prohibits production testing and requires responsible disclosure.
- Opire's [lifecycle documentation](https://docs.opire.dev/rewards/lifecycle)
  puts the payment clock after creator initiation: after the maintainer reviews the
  PR and starts the Stripe checkout, the docs say the reward arrives in about
  **1–7 business days**. Its [terms](https://opire.dev/terms-of-service)
  still do not guarantee that a creator will pay, and payment goes to the
  developer's configured Stripe account rather than being held by Opire.
- Payout routing must remain owner-controlled: Opire/Stripe onboarding should
  be completed in the recipient's legal name; PayPal Mexico converts to MXN
  when withdrawing to a Mexican CLABE; Wise may provide USD account details but
  availability and payer acceptance must be checked in the user's own account.
  No passwords, OTPs, API keys or full bank details belong in a PR, email or
  this workspace. The local [payout routing template](../../opportunities/gpiw-waterpulse/docs/PAYOUT-ROUTING-TEMPLATE.md)
  records the safe sequence without storing sensitive data.
- The screenshot's very large Opire totals are board aggregates, not a promise
  that one task pays that amount. A live detail page can still show several
  small rewards, multiple solvers and unpaid historical claims; treat only the
  individual reward rows on an open issue as actionable.

  the GmsCore/RCS card displayed **$1,909,380**, while its detail broke that
  into 20 separate available rewards and six solvers; the `c1work` card showed
  **$1,365,960**, but that detail had 58 reward rows. Neither figure is a
  guaranteed single payout or a fast job.

  requires an Android APK, GitHub repository, demo video and pitch deck by 8
