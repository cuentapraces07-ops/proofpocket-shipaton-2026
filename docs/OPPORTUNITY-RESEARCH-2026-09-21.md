# Opportunity research — 21 September 2026

This is a read-only, evidence-backed planning note. It does not claim
eligibility, selection, revenue, or payment. No account, wallet, store listing,
or submission was created while collecting these facts.

## Primary route: RevenueCat Shipaton 2026

The [official rules](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules) show a
US$100,000 Grand Prize and a submission deadline of 30 September 2026 at
11:45 p.m. PDT. Mexico is not listed among the excluded countries in the
eligibility section, but the entrant remains responsible for local eligibility
and prize paperwork.

The normal categories require all of the following:

- a first public release on the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Samsung
  Galaxy Store during the submission window;
- a working RevenueCat SDK purchase or RevenueCat Ads integration;
- a public under-two-minute demo video, a 1024x1024 icon, and a 1179x2556
  screenshot; and
- a free trial or judge promo code for premium features.

The Next Gen category avoids the store-release gate but requires active student
status and a qualifying academic email, plus a public open-source repository
with an open-source license. Neither store release nor student status is
assumed here. ProofPocket currently uses RevenueCat Test Store products only,
so it is a prototype and not a valid submission yet.

## Secondary route: Amazon Developer Hackathon

The [official rules](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules) describe an
Alexa+ track with cash prizes and an October 23, 2026 deadline. RewardRadar has
local MCP/fixture coverage and a passing test suite, but no authenticated
Alexa+ registration, live public deployment, or submission has been claimed.
This remains a separate route rather than evidence that a prize is owed.

- XPRIZE's [official rules](https://xprize.devpost.com/rules) show that its
  2026 submission period ended on 17 August 2026; the prize headline is not an
  open opportunity now.
- The World's Largest Hackathon page is marked ended; it is not a current
  route.
- In-person or paid-entry events were not treated as guaranteed income. No
  entry fee, travel booking, wallet, or transaction was made.
