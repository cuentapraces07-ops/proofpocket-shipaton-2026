# ProofPocket — submission draft (not submitted)

## One-line pitch

ProofPocket turns scattered work evidence into a calm, private checklist that helps an independent builder know what is ready before asking for payment.

## What the prototype demonstrates

1. Add an opportunity with payer, value, status and deadline.
2. Capture a dated evidence checkpoint with a short source/detail pair.
3. Surface a clear `Ready` versus `Review` state instead of implying a payout is guaranteed.
4. Keep premium billing behind a RevenueCat entitlement (`proofpocket_pro`) using the RevenueCat Test Store sandbox; no real charge is started by the prototype.

The current build also lets a user add a private opportunity directly from the
Overview tab; malformed or non-positive amounts are rejected before they can
affect the tracked total.

## Intended categories

- **RevenueCat Design Award:** restrained visual hierarchy, large amount focus, and a low-friction evidence timeline.
- **RevenueCat Peace Prize:** reduces avoidable payment disputes for independent builders and small teams by making evidence and expectations explicit.
- **HAMM Award (conditional):** a transparent Pro tier for unlimited exports and encrypted backup; real conversion numbers must be collected after a compliant launch and must not be invented in the submission.
