# ProofPocket Privacy (prototype)

Last updated: 2026-09-18

ProofPocket is a local-first prototype. Opportunity notes and evidence
checkpoints are stored locally on the device; the project does not include a
user account, analytics service, advertising SDK, or payout processor.

RevenueCat is wired only to its Test Store sandbox to exercise the guarded
purchase path. No production customer data, payment method, identity document,
password, or payout credential is collected by this prototype.

Before any public store release, the owner must review the platform data-safety
forms, production RevenueCat configuration, retention, support contact, and any
third-party disclosures required by the applicable store and jurisdiction.
